## When to Use

Load this skill ONLY if the user has `active_injuries` in their profile. Otherwise skip.

## Injury Severity Levels

### MILD (Minor soreness, full ROM possible)
- Continue normal training, monitor for pain escalation
- Reduce volume by 10-15% for affected muscle group
- Add 1-2 mobility/prehab exercises

### MODERATE (Restricted ROM, discomfort)
- Reduce volume by 30-40% for affected muscle group
- Substitute compound movements with unilateral or isolation variants
- Add dedicated prehab/rehab blocks (5-10 min) before main session
- Example: "If shoulder injury → reduce bench press to lighter weights, swap for dumbbell work"

### SEVERE (Avoid completely)
- Do NOT prescribe ANY exercises targeting the injured muscle group
- Recommend rest or refer to medical professional
- Offer alternative muscle groups for training

## Modification Strategies

### Chest Injury (MILD/MODERATE)
- Replace barbell bench → dumbbell bench or machine press
- Add scapular stability work: band pull-aparts, face pulls
- Reduce volume: 3×6 instead of 4×8

### Back Injury (MILD/MODERATE)
- Replace deadlifts → trap bar deadlifts or leg press focus
- Add posterior chain stability: bird-dogs, reverse hypers
- Reduce pulling volume

### Leg Injury (MILD/MODERATE)
- Replace bilateral squats → single-leg variations (split squats, step-ups)
- Reduce range of motion: box squats or pin squats
- Focus on upper body and core

### Shoulder Injury (MILD/MODERATE)
- Replace overhead pressing → landmine press or neutral-grip press
- Reduce horizontal pressing volume
- Add shoulder external rotation drills

## Example: Post-Injury Recovery Week Plan

For MODERATE shoulder injury during ACCUMULATION week:
- Week 1-2: Focus legs + core, upper body is low volume
- Target RPE: 5.5-6.5 (easier than normal ACCUMULATION)
- Add 2× prehab sessions (mobility + stability)
- Gradually reintroduce pressing in Week 3-4 if pain-free
